Cairn: when an agent needs your call, the decision comes to you

This is a prototype of an e-ink personal assistant. We are not trying to add one more notification channel. We are testing whether parking an agent's decision in one fixed place fits your attention better than a phone push when you have stepped away.

Below are its three cards. If you can spare 3 minutes for the form, it helps us a lot — and "I don't need this" is just as valuable to us. That is the answer we are actually hunting for.

First, what this actually is — so you don't answer the wrong question
  • A software-side demo of a USB dev prototype: it needs a computer attached. The cards below are software-rendered, not photos of a real e-ink refresh.
  • Standalone Wi-Fi, battery, and the magnetic mount are not built. Final thickness is unmeasured; 7.5 mm is a design target, not an achieved spec.
